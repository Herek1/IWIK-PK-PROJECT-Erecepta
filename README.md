Project: E-Prescription

Overview: The E-Prescription software is a digital solution tailored for companies offering medical services, medical institutions such as hospitals, and individuals accessing these services. This software enables users to easily access their electronic prescriptions on their devices, enhancing convenience and efficiency.

Purpose: Developed in response to the increasing demand for digitalization in the healthcare sector, the E-Prescription project focuses on:

Issuing medical prescriptions.
Processing prescriptions at pharmacies.
Enabling users to monitor their prescriptions.
This project simplifies the management of medical prescriptions, making healthcare services more accessible and streamlined for all parties involved.
